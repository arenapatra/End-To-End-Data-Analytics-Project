Excited to share my latest data analysis project: an end-to-end **OTT Customer Churn Analysis** using Python and SQL! 🎬📉

In the subscription streaming industry, retaining customers is just as critical as acquisition. For this project, I built an integrated relational database connecting customer profiles, subscription history, and support logs to uncover what drives churn and financial risk.

### 🔍 Key Project Highlights & Workflow:

* **Data Integration & Cleaning:** Extracted and merged multiple tables from a SQLite database (`customer_churn.db`) using Python and `pandas`, handling missing values, standardizing gender categories, and converting datetime formats.


* **Feature Engineering:** Created core analytical flags like `churn_flag`, calculated customer tenure days, established an ARPU of ₹18.85, and categorized churn risk levels based on predictive scores.


* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Discovered an overall churn rate of **28.57%** against a **71.43%** retention rate, and identified that Basic plans experienced the highest churn at **60.00%** compared to Premium plans at **14.29%**.


* **Correlation & Insights:** Found a strong positive correlation (**0.77**) between customer support escalations and churn, highlighting the critical impact of customer service resolution on retention.


* **Visualizations:** Utilized `matplotlib` and `seaborn` (including heatmaps, pairplots, and custom facet grids) to visualize monthly churn trends and plan distributions.
